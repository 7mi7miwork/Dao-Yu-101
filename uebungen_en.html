Exercises

Discover different formats for your learning – each type supports a different aspect of knowledge acquisition.

🧠

Understanding

Reading & Thinking

Text-based content that helps you deeply grasp concepts and theories. Through targeted reading and reflective questions, you develop a solid understanding of the fundamentals.

Example Task:

Read the text about cognitive learning theories and answer the following comprehension questions: What is cognitive dissonance? How does the theory of elaborative learning explain better retention for complex content?

In Preparation
⚙️

Applying

Step-by-Step

Practical instructions and work assignments that guide you through concrete tasks. Here you directly apply what you have learned and develop practical skills through active doing.

Example Task:

Create a personal learning map for your current project. Step 1: Define your main goal. Step 2: Identify required competencies. Step 3: Plan concrete learning steps with timeframes.

In Preparation
💭

Reflecting

Answering Questions

Reflective questions and self-reflection exercises that encourage metacognitive engagement. Here you deepen your understanding through conscious reflection on learning processes and personal experiences.

Example Task:

Write a short reflection on your learning journey so far: Which learning style suits you best? Where do you regularly encounter limits? Which successes would you like to celebrate and how can you learn from challenges?

In Preparation

Didactic Benefit

These three exercise types form a complete learning cycle: Understanding creates the knowledge base, Applying enables practical experience, and Reflecting deepens learning through conscious engagement. Each type addresses different learning styles and promotes sustainable competency development.

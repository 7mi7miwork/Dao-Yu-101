Roles

Discover which role best suits you and how you can optimally use our platform.

🎓

Learners

Actively acquire knowledge

Individuals who want to systematically acquire knowledge and competencies. They are looking for clear learning paths and want to be able to track their progress.

🎯 Goal

Structured learning with clear progress and verifiable successes.

📱 Usage

  • Follow learning paths from Level 1 to Level 3
  • Work through modules with various exercise types
  • Earn certificates and demonstrate competencies
  • Use community for exchange and support
Core Target Group
👨‍🏫

Educators

Professionally convey knowledge

Educators, trainers, and knowledge mediators who want to create learning content and support learners. They are looking for didactic tools and effective methods for knowledge transfer.

🎯 Goal

Effective knowledge transfer and learning support with didactic tools.

📱 Usage

  • Create and curate learning content and modules
  • Support learners and provide feedback
  • Analyze and optimize learning progress
  • Apply didactic concepts and methods
In Development
🚀

Self-Learners / Project-Interested

Independently expand competencies

Engaged individuals who independently implement projects and want to specifically expand competencies. They need flexible access to knowledge and practical resources.

🎯 Goal

Flexible access to knowledge and practical resources for concrete projects.

📱 Usage

  • Targeted research of topics and concepts
  • Application of practical examples and templates
  • Project-based learning without fixed structures
  • Networking with other project-interested individuals
